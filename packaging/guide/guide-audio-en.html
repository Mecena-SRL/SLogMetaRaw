Log MetaRaw Audio

Period recorders, microphones, desks and media · VST3 and Audio Unit · guide · v@VERSION@

Two plugins to record and mix the way it was done: "location sound on a Nagra 4.2 with a shotgun, mixed on a Neve, dubbed to VHS and captured back with a USB stick". Each machine opens on its own front panel, with its real controls and meter, and sounds as new: as it was heard then, not as it reaches us today.

The two plugins

Ripresa (recording)On tracks. Microphone → desk channel → recorder → medium. One instance per track: every instrument has its own microphone and machine.
Mix e supporto (mix and medium)On a bus or the master. Desk → master medium → dubs → capture → listening (radio, TV, jukebox, cinema).

Installation

Mac (Logic Pro, GarageBand, Fairlight, Ableton, Cubase, Reaper…)

  1. Open LogMetaRawAudio-@VERSION@.dmg and double-click Installa Log MetaRaw Audio.pkg.
  2. In Customize choose VST3 (Fairlight, Cubase, Reaper, Ableton, Studio One, Bitwig) and/or Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand, MainStage). Both by default.
  3. Reopen the DAW. Logic checks new Audio Units by itself.

They install into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components. To remove them: Disinstalla Log MetaRaw Audio.command on the disk.

Windows (Fairlight, Cubase, Reaper, Ableton, Studio One…)

  1. Extract LogMetaRawAudio-@VERSION@-windows-x64.zip.
  2. Copy the Log MetaRaw Audio.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  3. Reopen the DAW and rescan plugins if needed.

In three steps

  1. Ripresa on the track: pick the recorder, then a use preset (e.g. Cinema · Presa diretta, Sala di registrazione · Voce): the machine is set up as for that session.
  2. Play the track for a few seconds and press Regola livello (set level): the peaks go where the engineer put them, on that machine's meter.
  3. Mix e supporto on the master: desk, final medium, optional dub and capture.
The "?" on the front's nameplate: hover over it for the story of the recorder or desk.
The sound is built from published specifications, standards and period descriptions: it is an estimate of the behaviour, not a measurement of one unit. Where a value is inferred the documentation says so (docs/audio/AUDIO.md). Names and trademarks belong to their owners; the fronts are original drawings without logos. The panel is in Italian.

The panel

top to bottom

1 · The front

The real machine's controls, where it had them and with their labels, and working: the Nagra's selector goes to TEST, RECORD or PLAYBACK, the speed changes the tape, the filter, limiter, Dolby and tone act on the sound. Purely mechanical controls move but do not change the sound; channels the plugin does not use are off.

The meter

Each machine has its own, with its scale and movement, computed on the signal sample by sample:

VU99 % in 300 ms, 1–1.5 % overshoot (ANSI C16.5 / IEC 60268-17).
PPMIEC 60268-10 type IIa: 10 ms at 5 kHz reads −2 dB; falls 24 dB in 2.8 s.
ModulometerNagra peak meter; battery zone.
OthersMagic eye, neon lamp, LEDs, needle indicator.

In record and TEST the meter reads what goes onto tape (input × level), in playback what comes back. Taratura meter (meter calibration) sets which digital level reads 0 VU (−18 dBFS by default).

2 · Use presets

By category: Cinema (location sound, documentary, ambience, Super 8), Televisione (field report, studio, camcorder), Radio (interview and commentary, studio), Sala di registrazione (vocals, choir, guitars, bass, drums, piano, winds, strings, orchestra), Concerto dal vivo, In casa. The preset picks the microphone and its switches, mono or stereo input, filter, limiter and speed (the quietest for music, 19 cm/s for speech as in film), and writes below it the level to bring peaks to and the machines that were used.

3 · From microphone to medium

MicrofonoThe stock one or another of its era.
Filtro, DirettivitàThe real switches: MD 421 bass roll-off M…S, RCA 77-DX voice/music and pattern, U 47 cardioid/omni, the C12's five patterns.
IngressoStereo, mono (sum) or one side only.
Banco (canale)The desk channel the microphone goes into: it colours the track before tape.
Supporto, RiduzioneOnly what the machine accepted: a Nagra does not record onto VHS.

4 · Trims

Livello reg.How hard it goes to tape: low = more hiss, high = more saturation. Loudness stays.
Usura supporto0 % = new (as it was then). Higher: hiss, lost highs, dropouts, crackle.
Rumori, Wow e flutterMore or less than the real ones.
Panpot, Fader canaleThe channel in the mix.
Saturazione, Dry/wet, UscitaCharacter, blend, final level.

Recording and mixing

a band, a film, an interview

A band

  1. One track per instrument, Ripresa on each: the same recorder (e.g. Studer J37 for 1960s beat) or different machines, as in a studio.
  2. On each track the instrument's preset (Sala di registrazione · Batteria, · Basso…): it picks the microphone that suits the machine's era.
  3. The same Banco (canale) on every track (e.g. EMI REDD.51); mono input for vocals and instruments, stereo for overheads and piano; panpot and fader for the mix.
  4. On the master Mix e supporto with the same desk: it is the master section. Mix preset, final medium (e.g. ¼" tape at 38 cm/s), then optionally a dub (vinyl, cassette, VHS) and the capture.

A film or documentary

  1. Dialogue: Ripresa with a Nagra III or 4.2, preset Cinema · Presa diretta (shotgun, mono, filter, limiter, 19 cm/s). Ambience on another track with Cinema · Ambiente.
  2. Master: preset Cinema · Missaggio del film (mono), 35 mm optical track for the theatre print, or VHS for TV.

Automation: like a real session

Every control that changes the sound is an automatable parameter. In write mode (Logic: Touch/Latch; Fairlight: Automation) turn the front's knobs and the DAW records keyframes; levels, pan, fader, output and dry/wet move without steps. Changing machine or medium while playing can give a small click.

What level to record at

The guide under the preset measures the peaks of the last 8 seconds and Regola livello brings them here, on the machine's meter:

MeterSpeechMusicDrums
VU and indicators−30−5 (VU under-reads them)
PPM (BBC)PPM 5PPM 6PPM 6
Nagra modulometer−2 dB0 dB0 dB
LED0+3+3
Neonjust lights on the peaks

Period practice, not standards. On recorders with automatic level (Super 8 cameras, camcorders) the machine does it itself.

Capture

In Mix e supporto, after the dub: how the copy gets back into the computer. The player (home cassette deck, VCR, turntable with RIAA, aligned reels, projector, tuner) with azimuth, wow and hum, and the converter (USB stick, audio card, studio converter, 8-bit recorder) with its bits, noise and band limit.

The sound, as new

By default machine, tape and microphone are new. Tape records with its speed's standard equalisation (CCIR/IEC, RIAA on vinyl, FM 50 µs): highs arrive boosted and saturate first, as on real machines; there is the oxide's modulation noise; valve desks' transformers saturate in the lows. For the "as it reaches us today" effect raise Usura supporto.

The machines

common names, fronts without logos

Recorders (Ripresa)

Studio reelsAmpex 350 (1953), Studer J37 (1964), Revox A77 (1967), Studer A80 (1970).

Before tapeEdison cylinder phonograph, Webster-Chicago 180 wire recorder.

Microphones

Acoustic horn, carbon, piezo, RCA 44-BX and 77-DX, Shure 55, Neumann U 47, AKG C12, EV 635A, Sennheiser MD 421, Shure SM58, Sennheiser MKH 416, cheap dynamic, camcorder electret. Each recorder offers the microphones released within 15 years of it.

Desks (Ripresa and Mix)

RCA BC-2B (1950), EMI REDD.51 (1964), Neve 8078 (1978), SSL 4000 E (1979), Mackie CR-1604 (1993).

Media and dubs

¼" tape at 38, 19, 9.5, 4.75 and 2.4 cm/s; cassette type I, II, IV; steel wire; wax cylinder; 78 rpm; 45 and 33 vinyl; linear VHS SP and LP, VHS Hi-Fi, Video8, U-matic; 35 and 16 mm optical track; Super 8 stripe; AM and FM radio; telephone. Noise reduction: Dolby A, B, C, dbx, where the medium took them. Copie (copies): every extra dub loses highs and adds hiss.

Listening

Valve radio, transistor radio, Wurlitzer 1015 jukebox, CRT television, cinema (X curve).

What is verified

  • Verified by automated tests: meter timings per the standards, equalisations, filters, record level, presets, switches, capture; plugin validated with the Steinberg tool; builds on Mac, Windows and Linux.
  • Still to try by ear: Fairlight and Logic with real recordings. Fronts of Studer, EL 3300, TC-D5M, film cameras, Neve, SSL and Mackie rebuilt from descriptions where the manual was missing.
